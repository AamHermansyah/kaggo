# About MyKaggo

We exist because millions of Nigerians send packages without knowing where they are.

Across Nigeria, people rely on local buses and transport companies to send packages between cities. Once a package is handed over, both the sender and receiver are left guessing until it arrives.

MyKagggo is bringing transparency to the system by giving real-time updates on the package location, no matter the route or bus used.

## MyKagggo Works For Everyone

- Everyday Senders
- Dropshippers & SMEs
- Drivers & Transport Companies
- Corporates & Students

Built on transparency that protects everyone, MyKagggo brings trust to Nigeria's Logistics.
